How this works

Whenever a student saves a diary entry, the system automatically scans the content for distress keywords in English, Sinhala, and Tamil — even for private entries, because safety comes first. If concerning keywords are found, the student appears here so you can reach out early. Private diary text is never shown; you only see that the student may need support.

Why can't I see more?
To protect your student's privacy and encourage them to speak openly, MindCare never shares exact assessment scores or private session notes with guardians, and diary entries stay private unless your student personally chooses to share a specific entry with you (see "Shared Diary" in the sidebar). This status view only reflects general engagement — if you're ever concerned, please reach out to the student directly or use Emergency Booking.
